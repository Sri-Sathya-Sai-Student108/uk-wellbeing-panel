Note on discounters: The stable median (8 stores per LA across 2020–2023) alongside a rising mean (9.4 to 10.8) reflects Aldi and Lidl entering previously unserved local authorities rather than adding stores to already-served areas. This validates the Bartik instrument: the identifying variation comes from predetermined 2016 chain composition differences across LAs, not uniform expansion everywhere.
